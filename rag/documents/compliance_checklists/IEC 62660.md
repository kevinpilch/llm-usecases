#### **IEC 62660: Performance and Reliability of Lithium-ion Cells**
- **Capacity and Energy Testing**: Verify cell capacity and energy output under specified conditions.
- **Charge Retention**: Test the cell’s ability to retain charge over time.
- **Cycle Life**: Assess durability by conducting repeated charge-discharge cycles.
- **High-Rate Discharge**: Ensure cells can safely discharge at high rates.
- **Internal Short Circuit Test**: Confirm cell resilience to internal short circuit scenarios.
- **Thermal Stability**: Verify stability under temperature fluctuations to avoid cell degradation.
