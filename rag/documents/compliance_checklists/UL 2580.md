#### **UL 2580: Battery Safety for Electric Vehicles**
- **Dielectric Voltage Withstand**: Test battery insulation under high voltage.
- **Short Circuit Protection**: Verify battery protection against internal and external short circuits.
- **Thermal Abuse Resistance**: Ensure battery does not overheat under high-temperature conditions.
- **Fire Exposure Test**: Confirm battery resistance to external fire exposure.
- **Mechanical Integrity**: Test resilience to mechanical abuse (crush, drop tests).
- **Environmental Cycling**: Validate performance under environmental changes (humidity, temperature cycles).
