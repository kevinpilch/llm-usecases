#### **UN 38.3: Battery Transportation Safety**
- **Altitude Simulation**: Verify battery pack stability under low-pressure conditions (simulated altitudes).
- **Thermal Test**: Confirm battery performance within extreme temperature ranges (-40°C to 75°C).
- **Vibration Test**: Ensure battery integrity under vibration conditions similar to transport environments.
- **Shock Test**: Validate battery’s resilience to impact and shock.
- **External Short Circuit**: Test protection against short circuits in transport scenarios.
- **Overcharge Protection**: Verify that overcharge does not lead to thermal runaway or leakage.
- **Impact and Crush Tests**: Check that battery cells withstand pressure and impact without leakage or fire.
