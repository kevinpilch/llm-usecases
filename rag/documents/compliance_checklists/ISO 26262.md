#### **ISO 26262: Functional Safety for Automotive Batteries**
- **Safety Analysis**: Conduct Hazard and Risk Assessment (HARA) on battery pack design.
- **Safety Goals and ASIL Classification**: Define Automotive Safety Integrity Levels (ASIL) for battery functions.
- **Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA)**: Identify potential failure modes and preventive measures.
- **Validation and Verification**: Test compliance of battery functions with safety goals.
- **System Integration**: Validate correct integration of battery with vehicle’s safety systems.
