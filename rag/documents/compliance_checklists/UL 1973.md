#### **UL 1973: Stationary and Motive Battery System Safety**
- **Battery Enclosure Integrity**: Test for enclosure durability and resistance to environmental factors.
- **Component Safety Certification**: Confirm all components meet UL standards for safety.
- **Electrical Protection Devices**: Ensure all protection circuits are tested and functional.
- **Thermal Management Validation**: Test thermal management system for effective cooling.
- **Connection Integrity**: Check robustness of interconnections within the battery system.
