### **Battery Product Specification**

**Model**: CB-Me75

**Type**: Nickel Manganese Cobalt (NMC)

**Application**: Middle-Class Long-Range Electric Vehicle

---

#### **Capacity and Output**
- **Total Capacity**: 75 kWh
- **Nominal Voltage**: 370 V
- **Peak Power Output**: 120 kW
- **Continuous Power Output**: 90 kW

#### **Dimensions and Weight**
- **Dimensions**: 1,250 mm x 600 mm x 140 mm
- **Weight**: 320 kg
- **Form Factor**: Slim rectangular pack optimized for under-floor installation

#### **Performance Specifications**
- **Range**: Approx. 380 km (combined driving)
- **Max Charging Rate**: 100 kW (DC Fast Charge)
- **Charging Time**:
  - **DC Fast Charge**: 0-80% in 28 minutes
  - **AC Slow Charge**: Full charge in 7 hours (Type 2 connector)

#### **Thermal Management System**
- **Cooling Type**: Liquid cooling with temperature-controlled flow
- **Operational Temperature Range**: -20°C to 50°C
- **Battery Monitoring System (BMS)**: Advanced BMS with adaptive thermal management and voltage balancing

#### **Lifespan and Warranty**
- **Cycle Life**: 2,000 cycles (approx. 10 years in mixed-use driving)
- **Warranty**: 8 years or 160,000 km

#### **Safety and Compliance**
- **Certification**: UN 38.3, ISO 26262 (functional safety), IEC 62660
- **Safety Features**: Overcharge protection, temperature cutoff, and rapid-discharge protection

#### **Target Application**
Ideal for middle-class EVs focused on long-distance travel, combining high efficiency and rapid charging capability to support extended commutes and travel needs.
