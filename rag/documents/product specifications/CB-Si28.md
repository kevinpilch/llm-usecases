### **Battery Product Specification**

**Model**: CB-Si28

**Type**: Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4)

**Application**: Small Inner-City Electric Vehicle

---

#### **Capacity and Output**
- **Total Capacity**: 28 kWh
- **Nominal Voltage**: 310 V
- **Peak Power Output**: 60 kW
- **Continuous Power Output**: 45 kW

#### **Dimensions and Weight**
- **Dimensions**: 780 mm x 390 mm x 110 mm
- **Weight**: 160 kg
- **Form Factor**: Compact design optimized for small EVs with tight underbody integration

#### **Performance Specifications**
- **Range**: Approx. 130 km (city driving)
- **Max Charging Rate**: 50 kW (DC Fast Charge)
- **Charging Time**:
  - **DC Fast Charge**: 0-80% in 35 minutes
  - **AC Slow Charge**: Full charge in 3.5 hours (Type 2 connector)

#### **Thermal Management System**
- **Cooling Type**: Air cooling with conductive heat spreaders
- **Operational Temperature Range**: -5°C to 40°C
- **Battery Monitoring System (BMS)**: Integrated with automatic cutoff for thermal protection and voltage balancing

#### **Lifespan and Warranty**
- **Cycle Life**: 2,500 cycles (approximately 7-8 years in urban use)
- **Warranty**: 4 years or 80,000 km

#### **Safety and Compliance**
- **Certification**: UN 38.3 (transportation safety), UL 2580 (battery safety)
- **Safety Features**: Overcharge protection, discharge control, and temperature monitoring

#### **Target Application**
Ideal for small, lightweight EVs designed primarily for urban environments, focusing on reliability, energy efficiency, and fast turnaround for shorter commutes.
