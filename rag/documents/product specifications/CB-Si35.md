### **Battery Product Specification**

**Model**: CB-Si35

**Type**: Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4)

**Application**: Small Inner-City Electric Vehicle

---

#### **Capacity and Output**
- **Total Capacity**: 35 kWh
- **Nominal Voltage**: 320 V
- **Peak Power Output**: 70 kW
- **Continuous Power Output**: 50 kW

#### **Dimensions and Weight**
- **Dimensions**: 800 mm x 400 mm x 120 mm
- **Weight**: 180 kg
- **Form Factor**: Compact, flat-pack design for under-floor integration

#### **Performance Specifications**
- **Range**: Approx. 150 km (city driving)
- **Max Charging Rate**: 60 kW (DC Fast Charge)
- **Charging Time**:
  - **DC Fast Charge**: 0-80% in 30 minutes
  - **AC Slow Charge**: Full charge in 4 hours (Type 2 connector)

#### **Thermal Management System**
- **Cooling Type**: Passive air cooling with heat sink
- **Operational Temperature Range**: -10°C to 45°C
- **Battery Monitoring System (BMS)**: Integrated with temperature sensors and cutoff for overheat protection

#### **Lifespan and Warranty**
- **Cycle Life**: 2,000 cycles (approx. 8 years of daily usage)
- **Warranty**: 5 years or 100,000 km

#### **Safety and Compliance**
- **Certification**: UN 38.3 (transportation safety), ISO 26262 (functional safety)
- **Safety Features**: Overcharge protection, short-circuit protection, thermal cutoff

#### **Target Application**
Designed for lightweight, compact EVs optimized for city driving, balancing a modest range with energy efficiency and minimal charging times.
