### **Battery Product Specification**

**Model**: CB-Si30

**Type**: Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4)

**Application**: Small Inner-City Electric Vehicle

---

#### **Capacity and Output**
- **Total Capacity**: 30 kWh
- **Nominal Voltage**: 315 V
- **Peak Power Output**: 65 kW
- **Continuous Power Output**: 48 kW

#### **Dimensions and Weight**
- **Dimensions**: 810 mm x 400 mm x 115 mm
- **Weight**: 170 kg
- **Form Factor**: Slimline design for efficient use of chassis space

#### **Performance Specifications**
- **Range**: Approx. 140 km (city driving)
- **Max Charging Rate**: 55 kW (DC Fast Charge)
- **Charging Time**:
  - **DC Fast Charge**: 0-80% in 30 minutes
  - **AC Slow Charge**: Full charge in 4 hours (Type 2 connector)

#### **Thermal Management System**
- **Cooling Type**: Air-cooled with passive ventilation system
- **Operational Temperature Range**: -10°C to 45°C
- **Battery Monitoring System (BMS)**: Smart BMS with remote diagnostics and automatic temperature control

#### **Lifespan and Warranty**
- **Cycle Life**: 2,200 cycles (approx. 7 years in urban driving conditions)
- **Warranty**: 5 years or 90,000 km

#### **Safety and Compliance**
- **Certification**: UN 38.3 (transportation safety), IEC 62660 (performance and reliability)
- **Safety Features**: Overheat protection, overcurrent shutdown, and low-voltage lockout

#### **Target Application**
Designed for compact EVs tailored to urban settings, this battery emphasizes quick charging, efficiency, and reliability for short-range travel needs.
