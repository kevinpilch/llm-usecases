### **Battery Product Specification**

**Model**: CB-Me90

**Type**: Lithium Nickel Cobalt Aluminum Oxide (NCA)

**Application**: Upper-Class Long-Range Electric Vehicle

---

#### **Capacity and Output**
- **Total Capacity**: 90 kWh
- **Nominal Voltage**: 400 V
- **Peak Power Output**: 150 kW
- **Continuous Power Output**: 100 kW

#### **Dimensions and Weight**
- **Dimensions**: 1,300 mm x 620 mm x 150 mm
- **Weight**: 340 kg
- **Form Factor**: Low-profile design with reinforced casing for added safety

#### **Performance Specifications**
- **Range**: Approx. 450 km (combined driving)
- **Max Charging Rate**: 120 kW (DC Fast Charge)
- **Charging Time**:
  - **DC Fast Charge**: 0-80% in 25 minutes
  - **AC Slow Charge**: Full charge in 8 hours (Type 2 connector)

#### **Thermal Management System**
- **Cooling Type**: Dual-phase liquid cooling with thermal sensors
- **Operational Temperature Range**: -30°C to 55°C
- **Battery Monitoring System (BMS)**: Smart BMS with remote monitoring and adaptive load balancing

#### **Lifespan and Warranty**
- **Cycle Life**: 1,800 cycles (approx. 9 years of frequent long-distance usage)
- **Warranty**: 10 years or 180,000 km

#### **Safety and Compliance**
- **Certification**: UN 38.3, UL 2580, ISO 26262
- **Safety Features**: Overvoltage lockout, rapid cooling system, thermal insulation layer

#### **Target Application**
Designed for upper-class EVs aiming for luxury performance and reliability on long-distance trips, combining powerful output with advanced thermal management and safety.
